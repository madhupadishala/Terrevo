# Sprint 13 — Weekly Timesheet

Status: CLOSED

- **TRV-WTS-001** — An MR may generate one weekly timesheet per Monday-starting week from REVIEWED daily timesheets.
- **TRV-WTS-002** — Only reviewed daily evidence is aggregated; missing days are not fabricated.
- **TRV-WTS-003** — Weekly totals snapshot daily count, total/visit/unclassified minutes and call count.
- **TRV-WTS-004** — DRAFT or RETURNED weekly timesheets may be submitted idempotently.
- **TRV-WTS-005** — Managers require TIMESHEET_APPROVE in the employee organization scope.
- **TRV-WTS-006** — Self approval is blocked.
- **TRV-WTS-007** — Manager APPROVE or RETURN decisions are immutable history; RETURN requires comment.
- **TRV-WTS-008** — Missing-day completeness will integrate with leave/attendance in Sprint 17 rather than being guessed here.
